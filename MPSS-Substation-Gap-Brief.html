The MPSS answer

The MPSS is the deck that hosts that package — a standardized, four-column semi-submersible with an 8,100 m² deck and 10,000 t payload, leased on a dry charter. It carries the offshore substation, HVDC converter, or a full power-island (substation + storage + power conditioning) without the developer buying a $400M–$1B purpose-built hull.

  • Low motion is a cable-life advantage. Natural periods of ~24 s (heave) and ~42 s (pitch/roll) sit outside the 8–16 s swell-resonance band, cutting cyclic cable fatigue an estimated 40–50% — directly relevant to the €61.5B offshore-cable failure exposure (2024–35). Detailed cable analysis available on request.
  • Fast & standardized. Delivered 30–36 months from signature; serial-fabricated flat-panel construction (~18 man-hours/tonne vs. 150–180 for conventional semis).
  • Proven, not speculative. 35 years of independent validation — Lloyd's Register, ABS, DNV, W.S. Atkins, and University of Glasgow (Professors Faulkner & Incecik).
  • No CAPEX, no residual risk. You lease the deck and run your own topsides; we own, class-maintain, and insure the hull.
