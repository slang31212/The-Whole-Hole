Why the MPSS-O&G wins

  • Host off the critical path. Leaseable, pre-engineered hull compresses FID-to-first-oil.
  • SCR & dry-tree capable. Ample deck & stability margin; riser porches on the hull.
  • FPSO-class storage optional — up to 2 M bbl (SWIS) where there's no pipeline offtake.
  • Room to grow. Skid-on modules for tiebacks, compression, produced water, debottlenecking.

Built for

  • Deepwater E&P operators
  • EPC contractors needing a predictable production host
  • Teams wanting second-life power / CCS / hydrogen optionality
