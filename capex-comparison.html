Lease vs. build · The capital decision

CAPEX is the driver — and it isn't close.

Upfront capital committed to secure a deepwater production host, before a single barrel is sold.

Same job — host and process a deepwater field. One path locks up ~$1.5 billion of capital years before first oil; owning an MPSS needs $45 million (~33× less), and leasing needs almost none — the cost converts to a day rate paid out of the cash flow the field itself generates.

Why so much less? It isn't just the hull. A conventional host moves, so its oil/gas processing must be built as costly, motion-tolerant "marineized" equipment. The MPSS is engineered to be near-motionless, so it runs standard, land-type topsides — the hull and the process plant each cost a fraction of a bespoke build.
