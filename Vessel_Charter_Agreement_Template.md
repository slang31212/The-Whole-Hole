- The vessel shall be used only for lawful recreational purposes.
- Charterer and guests shall comply with all instructions from the captain/crew and all applicable maritime, customs, and immigration laws.
- ☐ Smoking allowed (designated areas only) ☐ Pets allowed ☐ Children under [AGE] permitted
- Owner reserves the right to terminate the charter without refund if Charterer or guests engage in illegal activity, endanger the vessel or crew, or materially breach this Agreement.

## 12. Vessel Condition and Inspection

Owner warrants the vessel is seaworthy, properly licensed/documented, and equipped with all required safety equipment at embarkation. A joint inspection checklist (Exhibit B) shall be completed and signed by both Parties (or Owner's representative) at embarkation and disembarkation.

## 13. Insurance and Liability

- Owner shall maintain hull and protection & indemnity (P&I) insurance covering the vessel and crew for the duration of the charter.
- Charterer shall maintain (or be added as additional insured under) marine liability insurance of at least $[AMOUNT] for bareboat charters; for crewed charters, Owner's policy shall name Charterer as an additional insured for the charter period, subject to standard exclusions.
- Charterer assumes the ordinary risks of maritime travel and agrees to indemnify Owner for claims arising from Charterer's or guests' negligence, except to the extent caused by Owner's or crew's gross negligence or willful misconduct.
- Owner's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total charter fee paid, except as otherwise required by law.

## 14. Damage, Loss, or Breakdown

1. Charterer/captain shall immediately notify Owner at [EMERGENCY CONTACT NUMBER] of any damage, injury, or mechanical failure.
2. If the vessel becomes unseaworthy or unable to complete the itinerary due to mechanical failure not caused by Charterer, Owner shall, at its option, repair the vessel, provide a substitute vessel, or refund a pro-rated portion of the charter fee for lost time — this shall be Charterer's sole remedy.
3. Damage caused by Charterer's or guests' negligence shall be deducted from the security deposit, with any excess billed to Charterer.

## 15. Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disaster, government restriction, or labor disputes. In such event, the Parties shall work in good faith to reschedule the charter or agree on a pro-rated refund per Section 7.
