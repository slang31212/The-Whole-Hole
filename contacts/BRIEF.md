## On the email column

It's deliberately empty. Individual executive emails at these companies are not published anywhere
public, and the broker sites that claim to know the patterns contradict each other — one search
returned three incompatible formats for Equinor alone (`FLast@` 51%, `first.last@` 98%,
`finitial+last@` 25.5%).

Guessed addresses on a list like this cost more than they save: bounces degrade your sending domain
before you reach target #10, and a wrong-person hit burns an intro you only get once. The CSV gives
your enrichment tool the two fields it actually needs — verified full name and verified company
domain — and lets it do the SMTP verification it exists to do.

Names, titles and divisions in the CSV are sourced; every row carries its `evidence_url`.
