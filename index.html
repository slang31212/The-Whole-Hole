The MPSS

Not new technology — proven technology, applied properly.

The Multi-Purpose Semi-Submersible uses standard ship-building methods: uniform box construction in stiffened mild-steel plate, assembled as pontoon, columns and a buoyant deck box with right-angle joints that are simple to weld and easy to inspect. It is less complex than other semi-submersibles, with excellent structural continuity — and it has been through extensive R&D, tank testing and independent classification review.

A standard hull, any mission

The same validated hull is adapted by its topside package — drilling, production, storage, accommodation or construction support. One design; many jobs.

Independently classed

Approved by Lloyd's Register, the American Bureau of Shipping and DNV — covering primary structure, intact & damaged stability, mooring and ballast systems.

Low-motion by design

A deep-draft ring pontoon sees roughly 40% of surface wave height at 27 m, giving very low heave response — effectively motionless in sub-12 m seas.

The Design Philosophy

The Five S.

Every advantage of the MPSS traces back to five principles.

S

Simple

Modular, panel-line fabrication using common shipyard practice. Standard mild steel, no exotic procedures. Single operating draft with simple ballast and trim systems. Right-angle joints are accessible and easy to inspect.

S

Safe

A massive reserve of buoyancy — a "safety belt" of ballast at sea level. Watertight subdivision throughout and inherent flood-damage capability. Tested to a 37.5 m (123 ft) storm wave with no water on deck, no emergency draft, and broken-mooring survival.

S

Swift

A modular panel-line design means a hull delivered in about 14 months. Quality assurance is straightforward, and prefabricated topside modules skid on before deck assembly — enabling fast turnaround during retrofit.

S

Size

In the ocean, bigger is better. Bigger is more stable, holds more, stores more oil, and can be reconfigured. Ample deck and stability margin support steel catenary risers and dry trees.

S

Saves

Low initial cost, low maintenance and low operating cost through standardized box construction — plus high residual value. The sooner it's built, the sooner it makes money.

The Model

Deepwater value is won or lost between host selection and first production.

Every deepwater development runs the same gauntlet before a single barrel is sold. The stretch from FID to First Production is where operators bleed money — and where a standard, pre-engineered host changes the economics.

The highlighted phases are the "kill zone" — where the MPSS compresses the timeline.

The conventional path

  • A new host engineered around every field
  • Long, custom FEED & risk-reduction cycle
  • Heavy long-lead procurement
  • Bespoke, engineering-intensive fabrication
  • Billions committed at FID before host risk is settled
  • Revenue delayed by host delivery

The MPSS path

  • A validated standard host — classed and known
  • Known hull, known motions, known fabrication
  • The host becomes leaseable inventory before FID
  • Repeatable, predictable build
  • Lease structure reduces upfront capital exposure
  • Faster availability accelerates first oil
The conventional model designs a new host around every field. The MPSS reverses it — standard host first, field-specific topsides second. That is the difference between a product and a project.

Heritage & Validation

Four decades of engineering, independently endorsed.

The MPSS was first proposed in 1980 by Craig Lang while working on the Conoco Hutton TLP. Since then its principles have been analyzed, tank-tested, classed and published — and the engineers behind it have contributed to some of the world's largest deepwater platforms.

Independent endorsement

  • Classification review by Lloyd's Register, ABS & DNV
  • Design evaluated and endorsed by Prof. Douglas Faulkner, University of Glasgow
  • Peer-reviewed: "Design Evaluation of a Multi-Purpose Semisubmersible" (Faulkner, Incecik & Lang)
  • Tank-tested at 1:100 scale; studied at Glasgow & Newcastle universities

Proven design heritage

Seaways engineers have served as consultant design engineers on landmark deepwater developments — and the MPSS configuration's principles are reflected across the industry.

The Business

Leaseable offshore infrastructure — not a one-off build.

For deepwater, the host hull is effectively long-lead equipment. The MPSS converts it from a custom project item into available leased infrastructure. A premium lease rate can be paid for by accelerated revenue alone — if first oil is pulled forward even by months.

Flexible commercial structures

  • Time-charter & bareboat lease
  • Joint venture & risk-share
  • Lease-to-own and outright sale
  • Lease-after-lease redeployment

Second-life & residual value

The MPSS keeps accepting work after first production — added tiebacks, extra modules, brownfield debottlenecking, power, compression, CCS, hydrogen, or float-off / float-on redeployment to a new field. That optionality supports high residual value.
