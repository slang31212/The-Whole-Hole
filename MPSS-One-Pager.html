Seaways Engineering · MILC — MPSS Infrastructure Leasing Company

Standardized floating offshore infrastructure, leased

The product is time and capital efficiency. The hull is the mechanism.

Customers replace hundreds of millions — often billions — of bespoke offshore capital with a standardized, proven hull they lease. MILC owns that infrastructure for decades and captures a fraction of the savings it creates.

Offshore development has always meant committing enormous capital to a one-off floating asset, years before first production. MILC breaks that model: the operator leases a standardized hull instead of building one — freeing capital, accelerating first production, and shedding end-of-life ownership. It is the aircraft-, FPSO-, rail- and shipping-leasing model applied to offshore infrastructure: customers buy production, not steel.

The ask — first unit

~$45M hull + ~$5M development. Sign the anchor lease first, then finance the hull against that charter (the FPSO template — the lender underwrites the contract, not the concept). One unit generates ~$120M/yr of operating lease cash flow at mid day-rate; a five-unit fleet, ~$600M/yr.

MILC is not building another offshore platform. It is standardizing floating offshore infrastructure and applying a proven long-term leasing model to an industry that has always relied on bespoke capital projects. Customers reduce capital, accelerate delivery, and avoid asset ownership; MILC builds a fleet of long-life assets leased repeatedly over decades. The product is time and capital efficiency.
