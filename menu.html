Configure a barge

The menu.

One basic barge, fitted out à la carte. The standardized hull serves seven or eight offshore disciplines — the only thing that changes is what you bolt on. Pick a discipline to load its recipe, add or drop modules — turbine handling, HVDC, BESS, a landing package — then size the fleet. The per-vessel and fleet economics recompute instantly. Every price is an editable placeholder, not a quote; your changes are saved in this browser and export to a spreadsheet.

Choose a discipline

A discipline loads a recommended set of modules. From there, tick anything on or off à la carte — go off-recipe and the build simply reads as a custom fit-out.
