Illustrative and transparent — not a re-run of AUPEC's proprietary model and not investment advice. Method: a simplified single-field DCF. Capex is spread over the development period; production follows a ramp / plateau / decline profile across the producing life; government take is applied post-tax with capex relief (so it scales the prize and leaves project IRR unchanged); the only difference between the two columns is host cost. Breakeven is the flat oil price at which post-tax NPV@10% = 0. Real figures need an operator's actual field data, schedule and fiscal position.
