Why this fits a family office

  • A hard asset, not a paper bet. Steel you can inspect, insure, mortgage, and redeploy — capital preservation with an inflation-linked, contracted income stream.
  • Cash from day one. No J-curve. A signed charter pays a day rate the moment the hull delivers — distributions begin early, not after a decade.
  • Patient capital is the edge. Your horizon matches a 40-year hull. PE funds must sell in five; you re-charter the same asset for decades.
  • Direct ownership, no blind pool. One asset, one charter, one clear story — co-invest or own outright, sponsor capital alongside.
  • Downside is engineered, not hoped for. Contracted revenue on top, hard collateral underneath, redeployment value at the end.

Why now — and why it's de-risked

Deepwater is where the barrels are, but operators are forced to build a bespoke host and wait years for first oil. The MPSS removes that — already engineered, tested and classed.

  • Standard topsides, not marine-rated. Its near-motionless hull runs ordinary land-type process gear — no costly "marineization" — so hull and topsides both cost a fraction of a bespoke build.
  • Standard shipyard steel — a host for ~$45M and ~14 months, not $600M+ and years.
  • Approved by Lloyd's Register, ABS & DNV; tank-tested to a 37.5 m survival wave, peer-reviewed and academically endorsed.

Most-likely maritime market

Deepwater oil & gas production hosting. The proven, bankable beachhead — long charters to creditworthy operators, a multi-billion-dollar leased-FPU market today (Modec, SBM, Bumi Armada). The same hull then re-charters into drilling support, offshore-wind install, power, CCS, hydrogen and data centers — one asset, many revenue lives.

The ask

Anchor equity for the first standardized MPSS host — direct co-investment or outright ownership — against a signed or advanced-stage charter, then compound as each host's cash flow funds the next. Target profile: sub-2-year payback, ~60% unlevered cash yield, hard-asset downside and multi-decade redeployment upside.
