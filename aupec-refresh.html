Independent Validation · 2026 Refresh

The 1989 finding, in today's numbers.

In November 1989, Aberdeen University Petroleum & Economic Consultants (AUPEC), directed by Prof. Alexander G. Kemp, independently modelled the MPSS across the North Sea and found it “anticipated to cost a fraction of a conventional purpose-built semi-submersible” — lifting post-tax returns across five fields and turning marginal ones into attractive investments. This note re-runs that comparison at today's oil price and fiscal terms. It is illustrative and transparent — not a re-run of AUPEC's proprietary model.

The Finding

AUPEC's conclusion holds in 2026 — and the prize is bigger.

At today's oil price and North Sea take, across the modelled fields the MPSS:

  • Lifts project IRR by ~8–13 points
  • Adds ~$75–180M of post-tax NPV per field
  • Cuts the breakeven oil price by ~$6–19/bbl
  • Turns an uneconomic small field economic — as in 1989
The mechanism is price- and tax-agnostic: a cheaper host lowers the breakeven, so more fields clear the bar. Higher oil prices make the absolute prize larger; higher taxes shrink it but do not change the direction. The 1989 conclusion is, if anything, more relevant to today's smaller, tighter fields.
