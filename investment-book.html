04 Why motion matters

Every millimetre of motion has a cost.

Motion is not an engineering footnote — it is the single variable that drives the whole lifecycle cost of an offshore development.

Higher motion

  • Higher cable bending
  • Higher fatigue
  • More failures
  • More repairs
  • Higher downtime
  • Higher LCOE

Lower motion

  • Lower cable bending
  • Lower fatigue
  • Longer life
  • Fewer repairs
  • Higher availability
  • Lower lifecycle cost

The same wave climate, two outcomes: higher motion compounds cost — lower motion compounds value.
